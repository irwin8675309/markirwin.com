OPENCODE PROJECT: PETS GAME
A Super Fun Adventure with Ghosty, Spin & Oakley

Build a polished, colorful, interactive browser game called Pets Game.

The game is made especially for two young girls, ages 3 and 5. It features their real pets as the playable characters so they can go on magical adventures together with:

- Ghosty – the white cat
- Spin – the black-and-white cat
- Oakley – the black dog

These must be original, adorable cartoon versions that clearly resemble the real pets shown in the reference photos. The goal is a warm, joyful, age-appropriate game that feels personal and special.

Primary platform: iPad Safari
Secondary platforms: Desktop, laptop, and mobile browsers.

────────────────────────────────────────

1. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & ORIGINALITY

This is an independent, original family game.

- All characters, artwork, environments, animations, and sounds must be completely original.
- Do not reference or imitate any existing television show, movie, video game, or franchise.
- Do not create characters that resemble recognizable fictional characters.
- No superhero costumes, capes, masks, emblems, or special powers of any kind.
- The three pets should look like cute, friendly cartoon versions of the real animals (big expressive eyes, soft rounded shapes, happy faces).
- The game name is simply Pets Game.

────────────────────────────────────────

2. VISUAL DESIGN AND ART DIRECTION

Create a beautiful, colorful, high-quality cartoon game suitable for preschoolers.

Style goals:
- Adorable original cartoon animals with large expressive eyes and soft, rounded shapes
- Bright pastel colors and warm lighting
- Magical but gentle fantasy environments (flowers, butterflies, soft clouds, rainbows, stars)
- Friendly, non-threatening obstacles
- Smooth, polished animations
- High-quality illustrated backgrounds

Prefer original SVG, CSS artwork, and Phaser/Canvas graphics. Avoid generic placeholder art.

The overall feeling should be warm, cozy, and magical — like a storybook come to life.

────────────────────────────────────────

3. PLAYABLE CHARACTERS – DETAILED DESCRIPTIONS

Create original cartoon versions that clearly look like the real pets.

Ghosty (white cat)
- Mostly pure soft white fur
- Light peachy-orange (flame-point style) coloring on the ears and very faintly on the face
- Large blue-gray eyes
- Soft pink nose
- Gentle, sweet expression
- Soft, slightly fluffy appearance
- Personality: calm, loving, and peaceful

Spin (black-and-white cat)
- Classic black-and-white tuxedo / bicolor pattern
- Black on the top of the head, ears, and back
- White face, chest, belly, and legs
- Distinct black facial markings (almost like a soft mask or patches around the eyes)
- Yellow-green / hazel eyes
- Alert, curious expression
- Personality: energetic, playful, and a little mischievous

Oakley (black dog)
- Solid black fur overall
- Some lighter gray/white whiskering and aging around the muzzle and chin (gives a wise, friendly senior-dog look)
- Warm brown / amber eyes
- Floppy ears
- Broad, friendly face
- Often shown with a happy open-mouthed smile and tongue slightly visible
- Personality: loyal, cheerful, and brave

Required animations for each character:
- Idle
- Running
- Jumping
- Landing
- Celebration / happy reaction

Children can select any of the three pets and change characters at any time. There are no special abilities or power buttons.

────────────────────────────────────────

4. MAIN MENU

Beautiful animated opening screen featuring:
- Large title: Pets Game
- Ghosty, Spin, and Oakley with subtle idle animations
- Colorful fantasy landscape
- Enormous, easy-to-tap PLAY button
- Character selection button
- Simple sound and music toggle buttons

Add gentle floating butterflies, soft moving clouds, and occasional sparkles.
No complicated menus or written instructions.

────────────────────────────────────────

5. CHARACTER SELECTION

Large, clear illustrated cards or portraits of Ghosty, Spin, and Oakley.

When a pet is selected:
- Highlight it
- Play a short happy animation
- Show a cheerful facial expression
- Play a happy sound
- Confirm with a large “Let’s Go!” button

Allow changing pets at any time.

────────────────────────────────────────

6. MAIN GAMEPLAY – OBSTACLE COURSE

Colorful side-scrolling adventure.
The selected pet automatically moves forward through the level.
The child only needs one large on-screen JUMP button.

Desktop support: Spacebar, Up Arrow, or mouse click = Jump.

Friendly obstacle types only:
- Colorful puddles (funny splash on contact)
- Soft fallen logs
- Gentle hills
- Floating clouds
- Butterfly gardens
- Soft yarn balls or colorful balls
- Rainbow bridges
- Bubble fields (pop by touching)
- Flower beds
- Magical glowing gates

Critical rule:
Hitting or missing an obstacle never causes failure, loss of lives, or a game-over screen.
Play a funny, gentle recovery animation and a positive visual effect, then continue automatically.
The game must always feel encouraging and positive.

────────────────────────────────────────

7. COLLECTIBLES

Lots of optional colorful items to collect:
- Stars
- Gems
- Butterflies
- Hearts
- Sparkles
- Ribbons
- Golden treats

Collecting an item plays a cheerful sound, shows a sparkle effect, and updates a large, easy-to-read counter.
Collectibles are purely for fun and exploration.

────────────────────────────────────────

8. ANIMAL RESCUE MISSIONS

Throughout the levels, friendly animals need a little help (bunnies, birds, puppies, kittens, turtles, ducklings, squirrels, etc.).

When the player approaches a rescue animal:
- A large animated rescue indicator appears
- The child taps the animal or a big Rescue button
- A cute rescue animation plays with hearts and stars
- A cheerful sound plays
- The rescued animal can briefly follow or wave goodbye

All rescued animals are saved to a simple Animal Sanctuary that the children can visit later to see everyone they have helped.

────────────────────────────────────────

9. GAME ENVIRONMENTS

Four distinct, colorful environments:

1. Rainbow Meadow – green grass, colorful flowers, butterflies, gentle hills
2. Enchanted Forest – soft fantasy trees, glowing mushrooms, sunbeams
3. Cloud Kingdom – floating islands, soft clouds, rainbows, stars
4. Candyland Valley – giant lollipops, candy hills, gumdrops, chocolate streams

Each environment has unique backgrounds, obstacles, collectibles, and a few rescue opportunities.
Levels should last approximately 1–2 minutes.

────────────────────────────────────────

10. LEVEL COMPLETION & CELEBRATIONS

When the pet reaches the finish line:
- Play a happy celebration animation
- Show stars and hearts
- Play a cheerful sound
- Display a large CONTINUE button

Award positive recognition based on participation only.
Children can replay any completed level as many times as they like.
No negative scoring of any kind.

────────────────────────────────────────

11. TWO-PLAYER EXPERIENCE

Design the game so both girls can enjoy it together.
Prefer simple shared or alternating play for the first version.
Both players receive positive recognition.
Avoid competitive scoring.

────────────────────────────────────────

12. AUDIO

Audio is optional and must not autoplay before the first user interaction.

Include:
- Cheerful sound effects (jump, collect, rescue, celebration, gentle recovery)
- Soft, gentle background music (different feel per environment if possible)
- Separate toggles for music and sound effects

Keep all sounds short, high, and happy.

────────────────────────────────────────

13. PRESCHOOL-FRIENDLY DESIGN (HIGHEST PRIORITY)

- No reading required
- Extremely large touch-friendly buttons
- No small icons
- No complicated menus
- No keyboard required for core play
- No time limits
- No lives
- No losing or failure screens
- No scary enemies or elements
- No punishment for mistakes
- No advertisements, external links, purchases, accounts, or collection of personal information

Every interaction must give immediate positive visual feedback.
A 3-year-old should be able to play independently while a 5-year-old still finds plenty to enjoy.

────────────────────────────────────────

14. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

Build as a web application using:
- HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
- Phaser 3 for side-scrolling gameplay, physics, collisions, and animations

Suggested project structure:

pets-game/
├── index.html
├── README.md
├── package.json
├── src/
│   ├── main.js
│   ├── game.js
│   ├── config.js
│   ├── characters/
│   ├── levels/
│   ├── obstacles/
│   ├── collectibles/
│   ├── rescue/
│   ├── audio/
│   ├── ui/
│   └── systems/
├── assets/
└── styles/

Requirements:
- Fully responsive
- Excellent iPad Safari compatibility (landscape orientation preferred)
- Large touch targets
- Prevent accidental page scrolling and unwanted browser zoom
- Smooth performance on older iPads
- localStorage for progress saving
- No backend, no required API keys, no external services needed to play
- Suitable for GitHub Pages hosting

────────────────────────────────────────

15. SAVING PROGRESS

Use localStorage to save:
- Selected pet
- Unlocked / completed levels
- Collected rewards
- Rescued animals
- Sound settings

Include a simple parent-only reset option that is not easy for children to trigger by accident.

────────────────────────────────────────

16. DEVELOPMENT PHASES

Phase 1 – Core Playable Game (must be fully working and fun first)
- Main menu
- Character selection (Ghosty, Spin, Oakley)
- Rainbow Meadow environment
- Automatic forward movement + large jump button
- Several friendly obstacle types
- Collectibles
- Finish-line celebration
- Basic progress saving

Phase 2 – Expanded Gameplay
- Remaining three environments
- Animal rescue missions + Animal Sanctuary
- Full sound effects and background music
- Additional polish and animations

Phase 3 – Optional Later Features
- Simple two-player support
- Light visual extras if desired

Do not leave non-functional buttons in the user interface. Prefer a working simplified version over an unfinished complex feature.

────────────────────────────────────────

17. TESTING & QUALITY

Before considering the game complete, thoroughly test:
- All menu and selection buttons
- Jumping and recovery animations
- Obstacle interactions
- Collectibles
- Rescue missions
- Level transitions
- Sound toggles
- Progress saving
- Touch, mouse, and keyboard controls
- Different screen sizes
- iPad Safari specifically
- Offline play after assets have loaded

Fix all obvious bugs. Prioritize smoothness, cuteness, and preschool accessibility.

────────────────────────────────────────

18. FINAL INSTRUCTIONS

Start by inspecting the project directory. If it is empty, initialize the project.

Build a complete, playable Phase 1 game first. Do not stop at visual mockups — deliver real working gameplay with character movement, jumping, obstacles, collectibles, and celebrations.

Priorities (in order):
1. Joy, cuteness, and clear resemblance to the real Ghosty, Spin, and Oakley
2. Beautiful original graphics and smooth animations
3. Extremely simple preschool-friendly controls
4. Always-positive reinforcement
5. Strong iPad compatibility
6. Clean code that is easy to expand later

Use creative freedom for thoughtful design decisions. Do not ask for approval on every small detail.

At completion, provide:
- Complete source code
- Instructions for running locally
- GitHub Pages deployment instructions
- Summary of implemented features
- Any remaining limitations
- Brief testing notes

The ultimate goal is a warm, magical little game that lets two little girls run and jump through beautiful worlds with their real pets Ghosty, Spin, and Oakley — a game they will love playing again and again.
